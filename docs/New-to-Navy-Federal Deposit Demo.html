New-to-Navy-Federal Deposit Demo

A prospect who has never banked with Navy Federal becomes a member with a funded checking and savings relationship, a working debit card and direct deposit on the way, in one connected story. The card assets we already built become the growth chapter at the end.

The story in one line

Who

Ethan Carter, Lauren Bailey’s fiancé. Eligible as a household member, banked with the same bank since high school.

His pain

Switching means moving his paycheck and every autopay. Every time it comes up, it feels like too much hassle.

What we prove

Navy Federal finds him, opens and funds his accounts with a banker’s help, and gets his card, paycheck and app working in the first week.

What Navy Federal should take away

More households become members

Data Cloud finds prospects inside existing members’ households, and one application converts them.

Deposits that are funded and used

Opening deposit, debit card and direct deposit are part of onboarding, not left for later.

Bankers advise instead of re-keying

KYC, identity and product selection happen on records the member already started.

A compliant trail by default

CIP answers, documents and verification results live on the member and the case.

Proof point from the lifecycle slide: connected journeys and personalized web experiences drove 35x more conversion (Banco Inter customer story).

Decisions before we build

Standard FSC objects for the deposit side?

Recommended. Open the accounts as FinancialAccount and track the application on ApplicationForm, and keep the custom card application for Act 5.

Banker channel

Video appointment fits the fiancé story and overseas moves. A branch visit is the alternative if the audience is retail operations.

What stays simulated

Identity vendor, OFAC, the external transfer and card processing. Say so on screen and show MuleSoft on the architecture slide.

Clean demo data

The org has two Ethan Carter members from testing. Keep one and reset it before each run.
